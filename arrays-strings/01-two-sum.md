## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**LeetCode:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a brute-force approach with two nested loops. The first loop selects one element and the second loop checks the remaining elements. If the sum of the two elements equals the target, their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1) excluding the returned array
